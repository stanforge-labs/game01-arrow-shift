# Roadmap

Current stage: product shell with Home, Levels and Game navigation, local unlock progression, procedural tactile SFX, Route and Rush modes.

Gameplay content is frozen: Puzzle (30 levels), Route (12 levels) and Rush (24 validated templates) are gameplay-complete. Procedural SFX, quiet ambient music and the local/Yandex SDK foundation are complete. Further work is limited to bug fixes, moderation fixes, cloud-save/ads foundation and release assets. The next approved stage is Prompt №22: cloud save and ads foundation; player data and advertising remain intentionally unconnected here.
