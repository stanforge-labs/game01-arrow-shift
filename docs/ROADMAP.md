# Roadmap

Current stage: product shell with Home, Levels and Game navigation, local unlock progression, procedural tactile SFX, Route and Rush modes.

Gameplay content is frozen: Puzzle (30 levels), Route (12 levels) and Rush (24 validated templates) are gameplay-complete. Procedural SFX, quiet ambient music, the local/Yandex SDK foundation, Player/cloud save merge and manual fullscreen-ad foundation are complete. Rewarded ads, purchases, leaderboards and forced authorization remain out of scope. The next approved stage is Prompt №23: production package, moderation checklist and Yandex Console metadata.
